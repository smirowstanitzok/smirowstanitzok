# Hi, I'm Sascha 👋

I'm a software developer out of passion and team lead of the **Middleware team** at [freenet Group](https://github.com/freenet-group), living in the wonderful town of Büdelsdorf near Kiel, Germany. At work I'm known as an AWS pioneer, with a lot of hands-on experience in AWS CDK and serverless architectures. These days I spend much of my time on AI in everyday development: coding agents, MCP and how teams can use them safely.

My motto: **Serverless Mindset. People first.**
